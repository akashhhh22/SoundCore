This page listens and draws the invisible pressure waves of your voice in real-time as a playful geometric blob.

The Anatomy of a Wave

Frequency (Hz)

The speed of the wave, perceived as pitch. Lower pitches render as warm colors, while higher pitches shift towards cool tones.

Amplitude (dB)

The height of the wave, perceived as loudness. Louder sounds push the blob further outward and trigger confetti bursts.

Timbre

The unique character of a sound. Voice isn't one pure tone, but many layered frequencies, giving the blob its complex, jagged shape.

Privacy Note: No audio is recorded or transmitted. All analysis happens entirely in your browser.
